## Metrics and evaluation

- **[C]** The LISI kernel `compute_simpson_numba` (evaluation.py:14) must not use `fastmath=True`: with numba 0.66 it collapses every cell to the constant fallback k (min = max = 90, fallback fraction 1.0); the corrected `@njit(cache=True)` kernel gave mean 1.92, range 1.43 to 2.00. The bug depends on the numba version. The comment at evaluation.py:8-11 records the removal. Any LISI number produced before the fix on an affected numba build is unreliable. [mem_6fc4988296f7 · observed]
- **[C]** cLISI convention (`clisi_graph`, evaluation.py:311): scores are normalised (lisi-1)/(n_celltypes-1), so 1.0 means cell types fully mixed and **lower is better**. iLISI and ARI are higher-is-better. `METRIC_DIRECTION` (experiment.py:496) encodes this. Confirmed on synthetic latents: iLISI 0.83 mixed vs 0.0000 separated; cLISI 0.0000 separated vs 0.83 mixed. [mem_d7902529e225 · observed; mem_d56e2434bcb7 · observed]
- **[A]** The scIB evaluation path (neighbours on the chosen latent, optimal-resolution clustering, ARI) was verified on a synthetic latent with 4 separated clusters: exactly 4 clusters, ARI = 1.0. Near-zero ARI in the rebuild therefore reflected the embeddings, not a metric bug. Do not re-audit the metric code for that symptom. [mem_63bb412301c7 · observed]
- **[C]** `asw_celltype` is the fastest "did the model learn cell-type structure" check: scIB rescales silhouette to [0,1] where 0.5 is exactly "no structure". Native-backbone runs sat at 0.492 to 0.500 on every dataset and both heads. [mem_96b93ca9b15a · observed]
- **[C]** `probe_metrics(adata, label_key, batch_key, embed_key, seed)` (evaluation.py:423) is the geometry-free conservation metric: cross-validated kNN (k = 15) and linear-probe accuracy for cell type (higher better) and for batch (residual batch signal, lower better), each as a lift over the majority-class baseline; subsamples to 6000 cells and drops classes smaller than the number of splits. Registered as `PROBE_METRICS` and wired NaN-safe into `full_metric_suite` (experiment.py:301). The batch probe doubles as a built-in "inert adversary" guard. Probe columns only exist for runs made after commit 91bf277. [mem_083c0ce41378 · observed; mem_4c46303ce684 · inferred]
- **[A]** The probe returns NaN (not a crash, not a made-up value) for a class with 2 members or a single class, and its majority baseline is correct under 95/5 imbalance (lift 0.000). [mem_d56e2434bcb7 · observed]
- **[A]** PAGA Spearman is computed by `compute_mean_paga_spearman` in `hyperparameter.py` and was added to `full_metric_suite` in commit fdfe6b8 (verified on real data). It is unusable in a sandbox with scanpy 1.9.8 + igraph 1.0.0 (crash on non-empty edge lists); scanpy 1.11.5 works. Expect NaN on simulated datasets with discrete groups. [mem_ca02d2e477c3 · observed; mem_5e349aacbfbc · observed]

## Adversarial heads and training loop

- **[A]** Sign and wiring (audited, do not re-litigate): the critic loss is pre-negated inside `ReferenceWassersteinLoss`; the generator's `all_loss = loss_vae - lam*loss_da` composes with it (generator minimises the Wasserstein distance; the discriminator path maximises cross-entropy). Barycenter anchors are owned by the generator optimiser and excluded from the critic optimiser. `z.detach()` in the adversary loop leaves encoder gradients unpolluted (tested). [mem_514dd47ccbc9 · observed; mem_d56e2434bcb7 · observed]
- **[A]** All three critic formulations (reference, pooled, barycenter) are multi-headed, C: Z → R^|V|, one head per batch; they differ only in the alignment target (a designated reference batch; the pool of all cells; learnable anchor points approximating a Fréchet barycenter). "Multi-headed" is accurate for the whole family; do not pin the critic to "reference" in text. [mem_0dd86730fd2b · observed]
- **[H]** E9 implemented the `formulation` axis (critic.py `ReferenceWassersteinLoss(formulation=)` and `multi_class_gradient_penalty(formulation=, target_samples=)`; adversarial.py `Discriminator(formulation=, n_anchors=64)`; commits 3324775 and 4a9ab59). The first E9 jobs ran on the old scCRAFT-derived backbone and were cancelled. [mem_8c17cfd3aa4a · observed]
- **[C]** Critic update count: `disc_iter` is 10 for the critic and 1 for the discriminator (user-stated convention); implemented at experiment.py:132 (`10 if critic else 1`) and recorded at experiment.py:648. This is why critic runs cost about 2.5 times more. [mem_14f0be5e7e64 · stated]
- **[A]** E9 `--arms` accepts exactly reference, pooled, barycenter, discriminator (`ARMS` list in `scripts/run_formulations.py`); `discriminator` is the reference-free control. [mem_5a02484f3e56 · observed]
- **[A]** During warmup the adversary forward pass and gradient penalty are computed then discarded (about 5.9 ms per minibatch); correctness-neutral, a code-clarity issue only. [mem_8b6d0bec728a · observed]
- **[H]** Regression tests for a silent adversarial no-op live in `tests/wcd_vae/test_adversarial_engages.py` (commit e84c8a2). Three invariants: the classification head alone beats chance on a separable latent; at lambda_adv = 0 the unopposed adversary drives `loss_da` below ln(V); changing lambda_adv changes `loss_da`. All three pass on synthetic data even when the real configuration fails, so they are not sufficient. [mem_8a01e707c0a3 · observed]
- **[C]** Real-data check that actually binds before any large fan-out: at lambda_adv = 0 assert `min(loss_da) < ln(n_batches) - 0.15`. It passed on unconditioned backbones (pancreas NB_uncond 0.995 and Gaussian 1.712 vs chance ln 9 = 2.197; immune NB_uncond 0.455 and Gaussian 0.397 vs chance ln 4 = 1.386). Log the `loss_da` trajectory and a batch linear-probe accuracy per run so a silent no-op shows in the results CSV. [mem_69fb240d087f · observed; mem_8a01e707c0a3 · observed]

## Backbones and primitives

- **[A]** `wcd/primitives.py` holds clean-room primitives (negative-binomial log-likelihood, Gaussian reparameterisation and KL, multi-class cross-entropy, seeding, batch-norm init, dataloader), validated against scvi-tools 1.4.2 (NB bit-identical, ZINB float32-equal) and scipy (`nb_log_likelihood` matches `scipy.stats.nbinom` to 7e-07, primitives.py:18). Only `primitives.py` was clean-room. [mem_b4e19cd53bb7 · observed; mem_d56e2434bcb7 · observed]
- **[A]** Provenance wording, audit-enforced: `training.py` (engine) and the `adversarial.py` Discriminator head were derived by modifying scCRAFT's `SCIntegrationModel` and discriminator and then substantially rewritten. Docstrings must say "derived by modifying scCRAFT, not a clean-room reimplementation", and must never say "shares no code with any third-party package" (flagged twice by the auditor). `backbones.py` native VAEs and `critic.py` are authored designs. The scCRAFT package was deleted in commit 6a2eff0; the latent key `X_scCRAFT` was renamed (legacy name persists in some functions). [mem_b4e19cd53bb7 · observed; mem_2fc8c602bd0e · observed]
- **[C]** `gaussian_sample(mu, var)` (primitives.py:39) takes the **variance**, not the log-variance (var = 4 gives std 2.0005). Passing log-variance silently inflates the sampling scale; check every new call site. [mem_252932f19615 · observed; mem_d56e2434bcb7 · observed]
- **[H]** Backbone conditioning defaults in `backbones.py`: Poisson, NB, ZINB and LDVAE default to `conditioned=True`; Gaussian and the `*_uncond` variants are `conditioned=False`. `_dec_input(z, ec, conditioned)` concatenates the batch one-hot onto z only when conditioned. [mem_16bae6e7cede · observed]
- **[H]** Mechanism found on real pancreas data: a batch-conditioned decoder removes batch signal from z by construction (batch linear probe 0.158 vs majority 0.228, below majority; min `loss_da` 1.774 vs chance 2.197), so the adversary has nothing to find. The unconditioned NB had batch probe 0.404 and min `loss_da` 1.047. The latent was otherwise healthy (z.std about 1.01, 0 dead dimensions, cell-type probe 0.70 to 0.85). The critic-vs-discriminator comparison is only meaningful on unconditioned backbones. [mem_2bb545a9b0dc · observed; mem_16bae6e7cede · observed]
- **[H]** Generalisable diagnostic: run at lambda_adv = 0 and linear-probe the latent for batch and for cell type against majority-class baselines; this separates "the model learns nothing" from "nothing is left for the adversary to detect". [mem_59066b684fb3 · observed]
- **[H]** Two independent defects were found in the native-backbone rebuild: (1) removed scCRAFT auxiliary losses explain only the conservation axis (ARI, NMI, ASW, purity); (2) batch-conditioned decoders break the mixing comparison. Unconditioned backbones fixed mixing only: ARI stayed 0.030 to 0.052 and asw_celltype 0.496 to 0.497. [mem_3a09ce063326 · observed; mem_18721e00976e · observed]
- **[H]** The triplet loss is textbook metric learning (relu of anchor-positive minus anchor-negative distance plus margin); what is scCRAFT-specific is the supervision (triplets mined from two Leiden clusterings at different resolutions, per batch, coarse labels vetoing triplets). Restoring "a triplet loss" is generic; restoring scCRAFT's triplet loss means reproducing their pseudo-label scheme, which has authorship implications. [mem_a5746c5ec4ee · observed]
- **[H]** To probe a trained latent, call the encoder directly (`vae.eval(); with torch.no_grad(): _,_,Zt = vae.encoder(X, warmup=False)`); `obtain_embeddings()` runs a 50-component PCA and raises when z_dim < 50. `load_task` lives in `wcd_vae.wcd.experiment`, not `wcd_vae.wcd.data`. [mem_2fc8c602bd0e · observed; mem_fd564a600624 · observed]
- **[H]** Embeddings were persisted from commit fdfe6b8 (plus dtype fix c12fb00): `evaluate_config(embed_out=DIR)` and `--embed-out` write one `.npz` per config (z, batch, celltype, X_pca). Reason: earlier runs saved metrics-only CSVs, so PAGA could not be added after the fact. Cast labels with `to_numpy(dtype='U64')` or `np.load` fails on object arrays. [mem_6ce853e6d3f3 · observed]
