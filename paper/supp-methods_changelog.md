## Corrections to the outline descriptions

1. Table S1 ("genes (2,000 highly variable)") and Note 3 ("input preparation: 2,000 highly variable genes"): the two
   chromatin-accessibility tasks are trained on all their gene-activity features, 3,429 (small) and 3,580 (large), not
   2,000 highly variable genes (`prep_scib_task.py`, scIB's full-feature setting for these tasks). The notes and Table S1
   say so; Note 1 already did.
2. Note 3 ("all metrics unscaled, raw 0--1"): the adjusted Rand index is corrected for chance and can be negative; two
   fits on simulation 2 (Sinkhorn at strengths 10,000 and 30,000, batch-conditioned decoder) score -0.00003. Every other
   value lies in [0, 1]. Note 3 states this.
3. Note 3 (blank metrics for chromatin-accessibility tasks): besides cell-cycle and trajectory conservation, highly
   variable gene conservation is blank for every task (not defined for embeddings), and the simulations also lack
   cell-cycle and trajectory conservation. Biology metrics per task: 7 (pancreas, lung), 8 (both immune tasks), 6 (the
   other four).
4. Note 2 ("KL warm-up of 400 epochs, incomplete on four shorter tasks"): scvi-tools counts epochs from zero, so the
   last-epoch KL weight is (E-1)/400: 0.9975 on the four 400-epoch tasks and 0.2025 to 0.6125 on the four shorter ones.
   The warm-up is therefore not complete on the four shorter tasks and ends one step short of 1 on the others.
5. Note 2 (input pilots, "-0.004, 9 of 12; -0.003, 14 of 20"): these are the values of the decision record recomputed
   after the Sinkhorn redo (`docs/prereg/r2r3_record.json` at 2168d75, branch final-review-fixes). The copy at tag
   10a1ca5 is the earlier record (cea50ca): -0.001 (12 of 12) and +0.001 (17 of 20). Both records keep the defaults;
   Note 2 gives both and says which is which.
6. Note 8 ("448 fits on the eight tasks"): 448 were planned and 447 fitted and scored; Scanorama with 80 neighbours on
   the large chromatin-accessibility task stopped with an infrastructure error (memory or a 32-bit index limit, per
   `D/sweep_analysis_final_20261105/RUN_RECORD.md`) and is reported as missing. None failed.
7. Note 8 (sysVI): sysVI runs with a Gaussian likelihood on scIB's normalised values and no KL warm-up (resolved
   `n_epochs_kl_warmup` 0 in every sysVI latent), not on counts like the other neural methods; scANVI's second phase is
   10 epochs on every task (min(10, max(2, round(E/3)))). Added to Note 8 and Table S4.
8. Table S1 (batches): the batch variables are scIB's, as prepared (`prep_scib_task.py` TASKS), not those of
   `configs/dataset_registry.json`, which is not what ran: immune 10 batches (variable `batch`, not `chemistry`, 4), lung
   16 (`batch`, not `protocol`, 2), human-mouse immune 23 (`batch`, not `species`, 2), chromatin-accessibility tasks
   `batchname_all` (3 and 11), simulation 2 its 16 sub-batches nested in 4 batches. The registry's note that the immune
   smart-seq2 batch is rounded at preparation does not apply to the `scib` count mode that ran (counts as distributed).
9. Table S1 (family and nesting): the human-mouse immune task holds all 33,506 cells of the immune task, and the large
   chromatin-accessibility task all 11,270 cells of the small one, matched by batch and barcode (the files add different
   suffixes to the same barcode, so cell names alone match only 3,618).
10. Table S2 (optimisers): the critics' Adam uses epsilon 1e-8 and no weight decay (torch defaults); the scvi-tools
    adversary's classifier uses the encoder's optimiser settings. Added to Table S2.
