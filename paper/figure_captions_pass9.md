## Figure 2
Ranking of the six penalties by biology kept at matched batch removal $b^*$. (a) Biology score at $b^*$ (colour) and rank among the six penalties within each task column (number; tied penalties share the average rank) for each real task, with the batch-conditioned and the unconditioned decoder. Hatched cells: the immune task with the batch-conditioned decoder, a low-signal pair that is not ranked. A penalty whose curve does not reach $b^*$ takes the bottom rank. (b) Mean rank over the ranked real tasks (five with the batch-conditioned decoder, six with the unconditioned decoder) per penalty and decoder; rank 1 is best; bars span the lowest and highest rank over those tasks. The pooled critic had the best mean rank with the batch-conditioned decoder (2.2) and MMD with the unconditioned decoder (2.17). The leave-one-seed-out standard error of each reading in (a) and the range of each rank over the five leave-one-seed-out repeats are in Supplementary Table S6. Simulations are ranked separately (Supplementary Figure S1).

## Figure 3
Batch score against biology score for the six real tasks (columns) with the batch-conditioned decoder (a) and the unconditioned decoder (b). Each line joins plain scVI (star) and one penalty's fitted strengths in increasing order, by mean scores over three seeds; no real-task curve ended early because of failed fits. Dashed line, matched batch removal $b^*$. Grey open symbols, each standard tool at its best setting by selection score (0.4 batch plus 0.6 biology) among the settings run; scANVI uses cell-type labels. Panels in a column share axes.

## Figure 4
Secondary summaries per penalty with the batch-conditioned (circles, upper row of each band) and unconditioned decoder (squares, lower row). One point per real task, in the order of Figure 3 from top to bottom within each row; black tick, mean over tasks. (a) Hypervolume of the strength curve (higher is better); it is not evaluable (n.e.) for the low-signal immune pair with the batch-conditioned decoder, so that point is absent and the mean is over five tasks. (b) Collapse rate in percentage points with exact 95\% intervals (lower is better); the mean includes the low-signal pair. (c) Local minus global batch removal at $b^*$; positive means stronger local removal; the mean excludes the low-signal pair. Ringed point, the low-signal immune pair with the batch-conditioned decoder.

## Figure 5
Width of each penalty's range of good strengths, in decades, one point per real task and decoder (batch-conditioned decoder, circles, upper row of each band; unconditioned decoder, squares, lower row; tasks in the order of Figure 3 from top to bottom; black tick, mean over tasks). A good strength has a mean selection score at least the lower 95\% confidence bound of the best strength; a width of zero means a single qualifying strength. Ringed point, the low-signal immune pair with the batch-conditioned decoder, included as in the Results text.

## Figure 6
Placement against plain scVI and standard tools with the batch-conditioned decoder. (a) Expected best selection score among $k$ settings drawn at random, mean over the six real tasks of each method's per-task curve (Supplementary Figure S6; selection score: 0.4 batch plus 0.6 biology; optimistic, because the best setting is chosen and scored on the same data). Each curve is drawn up to the largest $k$ for which all six tasks have $k$ settings: $k=10$ for five penalties and $k=12$ for the Sinkhorn divergence, and $k=6$ for Harmony and sysVI. Scanorama is drawn to $k=5$; its $k=6$ value (open marker) is the mean of five tasks because one setting on the large chromatin-accessibility task could not be fitted. Horizontal line, plain scVI (0.648); scANVI (uses cell-type labels) and the scVI adversary have one setting. At $k=1$ every penalty except MMD scored below plain scVI; the other penalties first exceeded plain scVI at $k=2$ (pooled critic, barycenter critic, Sinkhorn divergence), 3 (reference critic) and 4 (discriminator). (b) Batch against biology score for each real task at the single strength (penalties, Harmony, Scanorama, sysVI) chosen on other task families, or at the fixed setting (scANVI, scVI adversary, PCA with 10 dimensions); star, plain scVI. The unconditioned decoder is in Table 4 and the supplement.
