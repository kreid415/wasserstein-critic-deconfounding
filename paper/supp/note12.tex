\medskip\noindent\textbf{Supplementary Note 12. Direct biology check.}\par\smallskip
\begingroup\small\setlength{\parskip}{3pt}
\noindent\textit{Status.} The direct biology check is exploratory and descriptive. Its rules (\texttt{docs/BIOLOGY\_CHECK.md}, branch \texttt{biology-check}) were written on 5 November 2026 (commit 639c302), after the sweep scores had been read (the batch and biology scores of every sweep fit, $b^*$, the matched strengths and the label-transfer macro F1 at the matched strength) and before any value of this check was computed. It makes no new fits, runs no test and makes no claim of significance; every value is reported per method with its spread over seeds. The code is \texttt{scripts/biology\_check.py} at commit c4a793f, which made the run of 5 November; commit 697cc6c changed only the one-page summary and the script that writes it (the summary's seed spread had been averaged over all eight tasks instead of the six real tasks).

\noindent\textit{Fits.} For each penalty, decoder and task, the label-transfer strength of Supplementary Note 9 (the failure-free sweep strength whose seed-mean batch score is nearest $b^*$, ties to the smaller strength; the choice uses batch scores only), with seeds 100--102; plain scVI with both decoders, seeds 100--102; and every scored baseline row at each of its settings (the scvi-tools adversary, scANVI and sysVI with seeds 0--4; Harmony, Scanorama and PCA one run per setting). In all 783 saved fits on the eight tasks; low-signal pairs are included and flagged. The fits, latent files and their SHA-256 checks are those of the sweep analysis, read with its code, and the expression and labels are the prepared task files.

\noindent\textit{Neighbours.} For each cell, its 15 nearest other cells by Euclidean distance in the saved latent, the cell itself excluded. The same lists serve the purity and the smoothing below.

\noindent\textit{Readouts,} per fit and cell type:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \emph{Neighbourhood purity:} for each cell, the share of its 15 neighbours that carry its own cell-type label; per type, the mean over the type's cells; per fit, the unweighted mean over types. It measures whether cells of one type stay together in the latent space.
\item \emph{Rare types:} types with fewer than 1\% of a task's cells, with their purity and their label-transfer recall. They show whether batch alignment merges small populations into large ones. Tasks without a rare type report none.
\item \emph{Marker-gene preservation,} on the four RNA tasks (pancreas, lung, immune and human--mouse immune): the expression as distributed (log-normalised), restricted to its 2,000 highly variable genes; for each type the top 20 marker genes by the Wilcoxon test against all other cells, computed once on that expression and once on each fit's expression averaged over each cell's 15 neighbours; the readout is the Jaccard overlap of the two sets of 20. It measures whether the neighbourhoods a fit forms keep the genes that define each type. Types with fewer than 10 cells get no marker readout.
\item \emph{Label-transfer confusion,} from the classifier of Supplementary Note 9: types present in one batch only are dropped, each batch is held out in turn and its cells are predicted by a 15-nearest-neighbour classifier trained on the other batches; per type, the recall (the share of the type's cells predicted as that type) and the row-normalised confusion table. It shows which types are mistaken for which.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textit{Differences and summaries.} Each per-type readout is reported as its difference from plain scVI's value for the same type: for the penalties and plain scVI, plain scVI with the same decoder and the same seed; for the baselines, whose seeds do not match the sweep's, the mean of plain scVI with the batch-conditioned decoder over seeds 100--102. A fit's difference is the mean of its per-type differences over the types where both values exist. Per task, method, setting and decoder the check reports the mean and standard deviation over seeds, then the mean over tasks of the per-task means, separately for the six real and the two simulated tasks. Baselines with several settings are averaged over their settings. scANVI is trained with the cell-type labels, so its readouts are not label-free.

\noindent\textit{Checks that stop the run.} The latent's SHA-256 equals the one its score file records; the cell order and the labels match the task file; the number of fits per task matches the list above; every penalty and plain-scVI fit has its seed-matched reference; purity and Jaccard overlap lie in $[0,1]$; no value is missing where a readout is defined; and the macro F1 recomputed from the confusion predictions equals the label-transfer F1 of Supplementary Note 9.

\noindent\textit{Results.} Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:s10} gives, per method and decoder, the mean over the six real tasks of the purity difference with its seed spread, the rare-type purity and recall differences, and the marker-gene overlap with its difference from plain scVI. The per-task, per-fit, per-type and confusion tables, the record (with the SHA-256 of every input) and the one-page summary are in \texttt{docs/biology\_check/} on branch \texttt{biology-check}, with their checksums in \texttt{docs/biology\_check/MANIFEST.sha256}.
