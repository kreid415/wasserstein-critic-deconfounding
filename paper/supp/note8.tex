\medskip\noindent\textbf{Supplementary Note 8. Baselines.}\par\smallskip
\begingroup\small\setlength{\parskip}{3pt}
\noindent\textit{Methods and settings.} Six baselines were fitted on each of the eight tasks, from the same prepared files and training features as the penalties (Supplementary Table~S4). The \emph{scvi-tools adversary} is scvi-tools' own adversarial batch classifier on scVI (Supplementary Note~1): it runs with the batch-conditioned decoder only, and its weight is fixed at one minus the KL weight, so it has no strength to vary. \emph{scANVI} (Xu et al.; main-text reference) follows scIB's protocol: scVI is trained with the task's settings, and scANVI, initialised from it, is trained for $\min\{10,\max(2,\mathrm{round}(E/3))\}$ further epochs, 10 on every task, with the cell-type labels of all cells; it is the only method here that uses the labels. \emph{sysVI} (Hrovatin et al.; main-text reference) is the scvi-tools 1.4.2 implementation with its default prior and a Gaussian likelihood on scIB's normalised values, with no KL warm-up; its strength is the weight of the cycle-consistency loss, at 1, 2, 5, 10, 20 and 50 (scvi-tools' default is 2). The three neural baselines use the scVI-backbone settings of each task (latent size, hidden units, epochs, minibatch and training split; Supplementary Table~S2) and five seeds, 0--4. \emph{Harmony} (Korsunsky et al.; main-text reference) runs as in scIB: principal-component analysis of scIB's normalised values on the training features, then harmony-pytorch 0.1.7 on 10 principal components, with the diversity penalty $\theta$ (the weight of the penalty on batch--cluster dependence; 0 is no penalty, 2 the tool's default) at 0, 0.5, 1, 2, 4 and 8. \emph{Scanorama} (Hie et al.; main-text reference) runs as in scIB, with Scanorama 1.7.4 at 10 output dimensions and the number of neighbours used for mutual matching at 2, 5, 10, 20, 40 and 80 (default 20). \emph{Principal-component analysis} at 10 components is the uncorrected reference. Harmony, Scanorama and principal-component analysis run once per setting (seed 0); every Harmony fit runs in a fresh process with one thread, because Harmony was not reproducible from run to run within a shared process. Three further settings, at each tool's default size, are sensitivity checks and choose nothing: Harmony at $\theta=2$ on 50 principal components, Scanorama with 20 neighbours at 100 dimensions and principal-component analysis with 50 components. scANVI and principal-component analysis have one setting each, so neither involves a choice.

\noindent\textit{Fits.} The plan has 448 baseline fits on the eight tasks: 40 of the scvi-tools adversary, 40 of scANVI, 240 of sysVI, 56 of Harmony, 56 of Scanorama and 16 of principal-component analysis, the three sensitivity settings included. 447 were fitted and scored and none failed; Scanorama with 80 neighbours on the large chromatin-accessibility task stopped with an infrastructure error (memory or a 32-bit index limit, per the run record of the sweep analysis), which is not a fit outcome, and is reported as missing (Supplementary Table~S4).

\noindent\textit{Grid amendment.} The Scanorama grid was first set at 5 to 160 neighbours. A timing run on the human--mouse immune task then found that 160 neighbours did not finish within 8 hours and reached 43.9 GB of memory, while 80 neighbours took 2.1 hours and 23.2 GB. On 4 October 2026 the grid was changed to 2 to 80 neighbours, as the rule for this check, written the day before, required: an infeasible setting is replaced before the baseline grid is run, and the grid is not chosen from results.

\noindent\textit{Settings in the single-strength placement.} When each method is placed at a single strength (Supplementary Tables~\ref{tab:4} and S7), Harmony, Scanorama and sysVI get their setting by the same rule as the penalties: on each real task, the setting with the best mean selection score on the real tasks of the other task families, so that related tasks never choose for each other (Supplementary Table~S1). A setting is a candidate only if it was fitted on each of those tasks with no failed fit, and the sensitivity settings are not candidates. The task's own best setting, given in parentheses, is the optimistic bound.
