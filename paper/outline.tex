\noindent \added[id=P24]{\textbf{Background:} Single-cell batch correction often adds an alignment penalty (a discriminator, a Wasserstein-1 critic or a distance between batches) to a generative model, but penalties have rarely been compared on one backbone. We asked which penalty keeps the most biology at equal batch removal in one scVI (single-cell variational inference) model, and whether any penalty improves on plain scVI or on standard tools.}\par
\noindent \added[id=P24]{\textbf{Results:} In an exploratory comparison on eight tasks of the single-cell integration benchmark, we added a Jensen--Shannon discriminator, three Wasserstein-1 critics (reference, pooled, barycenter), maximum mean discrepancy (MMD) or the Sinkhorn divergence to scVI, fitted each at ten strengths from 0.1 to 3,000 with the decoder given the batch label or not and three seeds, and read biology at matched batch removal. The penalties were near-tied: 160 of 195 within-pair biology differences were under 0.02. The pooled critic had the best mean rank with the batch-conditioned decoder (2.20 over five real tasks) and MMD with the unconditioned decoder (2.17 over six); on five new seeds the four within-pair differences of 0.04 or more kept their sign, but the Sinkhorn divergence and the pooled critic led. Up to the highest batch score every penalty reached, the median collapse rate with the batch-conditioned decoder was 34\% for the discriminator and 3\% for MMD. At one strength chosen on other task families, the penalties scored above batch-conditioned plain scVI on 3 to 5 of six real tasks (median difference $+0.006$), and Harmony, Scanorama and sysVI on 1, 0 and 1; scANVI, trained with cell-type labels, scored above every penalty on every real task except pancreas.}\par
\noindent \added[id=P24]{\textbf{Conclusions:} On these tasks the penalties kept similar biology at matched batch removal and differed mainly at strong settings: the range between the highest and the lowest penalty had a median of 0.025 at matched batch removal and 0.213 at each penalty's strongest setting\added[id=P29]{, where batch removal was not matched}. Adding a penalty to batch-conditioned scVI gave at most a small gain over plain scVI.}

\hd{\added[id=P3]{Conclusions}}{\added[id=P3]{about 150 words; 1 paragraph}}
\begin{itemize}
\item On eight tasks of the single-cell integration benchmark, six alignment penalties added to one scVI model kept similar biology at matched batch removal: the best mean rank was the pooled critic's with the batch-conditioned decoder (2.20) and MMD's with the unconditioned decoder (2.17), but 160 of the 195 within-pair differences were under 0.02, so the ranking does not separate most penalties, and on new training seeds the Sinkhorn divergence and the pooled critic led instead. At the strength chosen on other task families with the batch-conditioned decoder, the penalties \replaced[id=P24]{scored above}{exceeded} plain scVI on 3 to 5 of six real tasks (median difference $+0.006$), whereas Harmony, Scanorama and sysVI \replaced[id=P24]{at the settings tested did so}{exceeded it} on 1, 0 and 1 of six.
\item Observations for scVI users, from the scores on these eight tasks and not tested on independent data: (i) the penalties differed mainly at strong settings, not at matched batch removal (Res-3); (ii) plain scVI with the batch-conditioned decoder remained a strong candidate (Res-6); (iii) the \deleted[id=P24]{biology was worth checking directly at the chosen strength, because the discriminator lost the most neighbourhood purity in the} direct biology check \replaced[id=P24]{showed small average changes from}{($-0.052$ against} plain \replaced[id=P24]{scVI at first-crossing strengths,\deleted[id=P29]{ without a consistent disadvantage}}{scVI, $-0.107$} \replaced[id=P29]{and}{for} \replaced[id=P24]{the discriminator \replaced[id=P29]{had the largest purity loss with the batch-conditioned decoder}{across decoders} (Res-5);}{rare types, batch-conditioned decoder);} (iv) when cell-type labels were available, scANVI scored higher than every penalty on every real task except pancreas (Res-6), but it is trained with the labels that several biology metrics also use, so its biology scores are not label-free.
\end{itemize}

they are fixed in advance and not tuned, and the MMD results hold for this kernel)}; debiased Sinkhorn divergence (entropy-regularised optimal transport; not W1 at the smoothing used\added{; Euclidean cost, entropic smoothing $0.05\sqrt{2d}$ for latent dimension $d$, float64, marginal error $10^{-4}$, cap of 500,000 iterations}); scvi-tools adversarial classifier (conditioned decoder only\added{; 32 hidden units, weight one minus the KL weight}); plain scVI as strength zero\added{; each critic head has two hidden layers of 128 units with one output per batch}.
