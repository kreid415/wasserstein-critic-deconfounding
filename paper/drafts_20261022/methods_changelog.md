## 4. Moved to Results (one sentence each, for the coordinator to paste; detail in Supplementary Note 7 or 10)

- **Trade-off area (Res-5).** "The trade-off area is the part of the batch-by-biology plane that a method's six points reach beyond a corner at plain scVI's batch score and 0.10 below its biology score, divided by the batch gap that sets $b^*$ so that tasks can be pooled (Supplementary Note 7)." Source: SPEC:146-157.
- **Collapse rate (Res-5).** "A fit counts as collapsed if it fails or its biology score is at least 0.10 below the same seed's plain scVI; the rate over the 30 fits per method, task and decoder has exact binomial (Clopper--Pearson) 95\% intervals \citep{clopper1934use}, and the reference-minus-discriminator difference is pooled with a margin of 5 percentage points (Supplementary Note 7)." Source: SPEC:221-230 via factcheck rows; paper.tex:406.
- **Local versus global removal (Res-6).** "Local removal is the mean of iLISI, kBET and graph connectivity and global removal the mean of principal-component regression and batch ASW, so the batch score is $(3\,\mathrm{local}+2\,\mathrm{global})/5$; at the primary comparison's crossing we ask whether the reference critic reaches $b^*$ with more local mixing than the discriminator, by an exact sign test over the real tasks and the pooled model with margin 0.01 (Supplementary Note 7)." Source: SPEC:237-246 via factcheck; paper.tex:407.
- **Good-strength width (Res-7).** "For each method, task and decoder the good range holds every strength whose mean selection score is at least the lower 95\% t bound of the best strength's mean; its width is the span in decades, descriptive (Supplementary Note 7)." Source: SPEC:278-281.
- **Single-strength placement (Res-9).** "In the single-strength tables each penalty uses the strength with the best mean selection score on the real tasks of the other families (related tasks never choose for each other; simulations take the strength chosen on all six real tasks), shown beside the task's own best strength as an optimistic bound (Supplementary Note 7)." Source: SPEC:250-256.
- **Best-of-k, equal tries (Res-10).** "Each method's expected best selection score among $k$ settings drawn at random, $k=1$--6, is $E_k=\sum_i S_{(i)}\binom{i-1}{k-1}/\binom{N}{k}$ over its $N$ settings sorted ascending; it is optimistic, because the best setting is chosen and scored on the same data, and scANVI is shown in its own label-aware stratum (Supplementary Note 7)." Source: SPEC:267-274.
- **Premise check (Res-2).** "Batch separability is the macro one-versus-rest AUC of a 15-nearest-neighbour batch classifier on the 80 plain-scVI embeddings (five-fold cross-validation; nearly separable if at least 0.95 in all five seeds), and each epoch of the 2,880 penalty fits logs the adversary's gradient norm before multiplication by strength and, for the discriminator, its batch accuracy; a gradient vanishes early if it stays at or below 10\% of its own peak from within the first 10\% of epochs; descriptive, no new fits (Supplementary Note 10)." Source: outline:198-205 (Meth-17, fact-checked against R0_X10_DEFAULTS.md by factcheck; paper.tex:430-432).
- **Label transfer (Res-6).** "For each method at its matched strength (chosen by batch score, never by this check), plus plain scVI and the scvi-tools classifier labelled unmatched, each batch is held out in turn and a 15-nearest-neighbour classifier trained on the others predicts its cell types; cell types found in only one batch are dropped, F1 is averaged over types, and the gap between the matched batch score and $b^*$ is printed beside it; descriptive (Supplementary Note 10)." Source: outline:206-212 (Meth-18; paper.tex:433).
- **Follow-up read-out (Res-8).** "Besides the confirmatory contrast, the registered secondary contrasts (regularisation within each family, optimiser within the discriminator, update steps for the critic, and the five-step gradient-penalty critic minus the one-step discriminator as used in the main benchmark) are pooled with the same rule but not Holm-corrected, every task is also reported at fixed strength (not a matched comparison), and the follow-up reports failure counts rather than collapse rates because it has no plain-scVI fits (Supplementary Note 9)." Source: SPEC-FU:241-246, 109-114, 219 (departure 7, no pooled collapse) and 157 (section title "Collapse and failure").
