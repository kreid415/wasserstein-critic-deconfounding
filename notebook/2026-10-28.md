## NB-20261028-05 · 2026-10-28T14:52:25-04:00 · ISSUE
**Every workstation LISI value rests on a hardware fault**
- author: agent
- problem: workstation LISI scores are the score of one fixed arbitrary half of the cells (seed 0xffffffff) and repeat only while RDRAND stays stuck
- impact: a firmware update or another machine changes all LISI values by about 0.005 (iLISI) and 0.004 (cLISI); the G4 cross-host iLISI difference was probably partly this
- next: user decision: adopt the preload library on JHPCE and the workstation (no score change), or change the rule (designed seed or all-cell LISI); record the RDRAND state in the score row
